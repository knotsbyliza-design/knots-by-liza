About Knots by Liza

Handmade, start to finish

Hi, I'm Liza , the idea and the hands behind Knots by Liza. What started with a love for crochet became this little business, where I get to turn simple strands of yarn into pieces made especially for you. Every bag, flower, plushie, accessory, or little creation is handmade by me.

I believe the little details matter. From choosing colours and keeping the stitches neat to adding those final finishing touches, I put care into every piece I make.
